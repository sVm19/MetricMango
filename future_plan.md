# Metric Mango Future Plan
### Practical Product Roadmap for 2026

---

# 1. Product Direction

Metric Mango should stay focused on a clear promise:

**Help small Shopify merchants know what to reorder, when to reorder it, and why it matters.**

This means the roadmap should deepen:
- restock confidence
- proactive alerts
- simple forecasting
- merchant actionability

It should avoid turning the product into:
- a full ERP
- a warehouse management system
- a manufacturing suite
- a broad operations platform too early

---

# 2. Roadmap Principles

## 2.1 Stay inside the wedge
Metric Mango wins by being:
- simple
- fast to understand
- easy to trust
- cheap enough to justify instantly

## 2.2 Improve retention through repeated value
Retention should come from:
- recurring weekly value
- clearer proof of ROI
- better alert personalization
- faster action from insight

## 2.3 Expand only after the core is strong
Do not add multiple channels at once.

If expansion happens, Phase 3 adds:
- Amazon only

Not included for now:
- WooCommerce
- BigCommerce
- Etsy
- manufacturing workflows
- accounting integrations
- enterprise developer platform

---

# 3. Phase 1: Deepen the Core

## Objective
Make Metric Mango more useful every week for existing Shopify merchants and reduce avoidable churn.

## Features

### 3.1 Custom alert settings
Allow merchants to configure:
- low-stock threshold by days
- low-stock threshold by units
- email frequency
- recipient email selection
- sales spike notifications

### 3.2 Weekly restock action plan
Send a weekly summary with:
- top SKUs at risk
- estimated stockout dates
- suggested reorder quantities
- highest-priority actions first

This should also be visible inside the dashboard.

### 3.3 Revenue at risk
Show the likely revenue impact of stocking out:
- per SKU
- over the next 7 days
- over the next 14 days

This turns forecasts into a clear business case.

### 3.4 Supplier lead times
Add simple supplier planning inputs:
- default lead time per product
- optional supplier name
- optional safety buffer days

This makes reorder recommendations more believable.

### 3.5 Purchase order export
Generate a lightweight reorder document or CSV with:
- product
- SKU
- current stock
- expected demand
- recommended reorder quantity
- supplier info if available

### 3.6 Dashboard clarity improvements
Improve the current UI using:
- stronger stock health hierarchy
- clearer urgent vs safe states
- cleaner action cards
- mobile-friendly spacing
- better visibility for the "next action"

## Expected Outcome
By the end of Phase 1, Metric Mango should feel less like a passive dashboard and more like a weekly inventory assistant.

---

# 4. Phase 2: Add Actionability

## Objective
Help merchants take action directly from Metric Mango instead of only reading insights.

## Features

### 4.1 Supplier directory
Create a lightweight supplier layer with:
- supplier name
- contact email
- default lead time
- notes
- linked products

### 4.2 Purchase order approval workflow
Support a simple workflow:
- draft created from forecast
- merchant reviews
- export or send manually

Do not automate sending by default in this phase.

### 4.3 Better SKU analytics
Expand dashboard analytics with:
- sell-through rate
- stock cover days
- fast-moving SKUs
- slow-moving SKUs
- trend direction

### 4.4 Proactive retention flows
Add retention features inside billing and product usage flows:
- pause subscription instead of cancel
- cancel reason survey
- save offers for low-usage users
- re-engagement emails when merchants stop visiting or acting on alerts

### 4.5 Packaging review
Keep the core plan simple, but prepare for a future packaging split by identifying features that could later belong to:
- Starter
- Growth

Do not launch complex tiering until there is real upgrade demand.

## Expected Outcome
By the end of Phase 2, Metric Mango should help merchants move from insight to action, which increases product stickiness and retention.

---

# 5. Phase 3: Careful Expansion

## Objective
Expand beyond Shopify without breaking product focus.

## Expansion Rule
Only one new channel is added in this phase:

- Amazon

No other channels should be started in the same phase.

## Features

### 5.1 Amazon integration
Add Amazon as the first non-Shopify channel for:
- catalog sync
- stock visibility
- order/sales data ingestion

The goal is not full marketplace operations management.
The goal is better demand visibility for merchants selling on both Shopify and Amazon.

### 5.2 Cross-channel demand view
Provide a simple combined view of:
- Shopify sales
- Amazon sales
- total demand by SKU
- total stock risk

### 5.3 Forecast upgrades
Improve forecasting quality with:
- seasonality adjustments
- better handling of sudden spikes
- confidence indicators

### 5.4 Annual billing
If retention metrics are healthy, add annual billing to improve cash flow and reduce churn.

## Expected Outcome
By the end of Phase 3, Metric Mango should remain simple while supporting merchants that sell on Shopify and Amazon.

---

# 6. What We Are Not Building Yet

To protect focus, the following are explicitly out of scope for now:

- WooCommerce
- BigCommerce
- Etsy
- multi-warehouse enterprise inventory systems
- BOM and manufacturing planning
- QuickBooks, Xero, and NetSuite integrations
- mobile app
- full API platform
- advanced autonomous procurement

---

# 7. Success Metrics

The roadmap should be judged by these outcomes:

- higher trial-to-paid conversion
- lower churn
- higher weekly active merchant rate
- more alert engagement
- more merchants using reorder recommendations
- stronger perceived ROI relative to price

Suggested product metrics:
- weekly action plan open rate
- alert click-through rate
- PO export usage
- percentage of merchants who configure custom alerts
- percentage of merchants who add supplier lead times
- save rate from pause/cancel flow

---

# 8. Final Positioning Guardrail

Metric Mango should become:

**The simplest inventory decision assistant for small Shopify merchants, then the same for merchants who also sell on Amazon.**

That is a strong product story.

Trying to become a full ERP too early is not.
