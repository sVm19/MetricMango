# Q1 – Immediate Impact Upgrade Prompt

**Objective:**  
Close the most critical competitive gaps by expanding integrations, analytics, and automation. Position Metric Mango as more than a Shopify-only alert tool.

**Detailed Prompt:**  
Design and implement the following upgrades:
1. **Multi-channel integrations**: Add support for WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Amazon, and Etsy. Ensure seamless syncing of product catalogs, stock levels, and sales data.  
2. **Advanced dashboards**: Build SKU-level analytics including sell-through rates, ABC analysis, profitability tracking, and trend visualization. Provide merchants with clear, actionable insights.  
3. **Purchase order automation**: Enable auto-generation of purchase orders based on forecasted demand. Include supplier details, lead times, and reorder quantities.  

**Expected Outcome:**  
By the end of Q1, Metric Mango should evolve into a multi-channel forecasting tool with actionable dashboards and automated ordering workflows, making it competitive with Inventory Planner and Katana.


# Q2 – Scaling Features Upgrade Prompt

**Objective:**  
Support scaling merchants with deeper operational features and integrations.

**Detailed Prompt:**  
Develop the following upgrades:
1. **Multi-location inventory tracking**: Allow merchants to manage stock across warehouses, fulfillment centers, and retail outlets. Provide visibility into transfers and stock balances.  
2. **ERP/accounting integrations**: Connect Metric Mango with QuickBooks, Xero, and NetSuite. Sync purchase orders, invoices, and financial data for streamlined workflows.  
3. **Custom alerts**: Enable merchants to set personalized thresholds (e.g., “alert me when stock < 20 units” or “notify me when sales spike > 30%”).  

**Expected Outcome:**  
By the end of Q2, Metric Mango should be capable of supporting merchants with multiple locations and integrated financial workflows, making it attractive to scaling businesses.

# Q3 – Differentiation Upgrade Prompt

**Objective:**  
Differentiate Metric Mango by adding manufacturing and supplier-focused features, plus mobile accessibility.

**Detailed Prompt:**  
Introduce the following upgrades:
1. **Manufacturing workflows**: Add Bill of Materials (BOM) tracking, raw material forecasting, and production scheduling. Help D2C brands manage both finished goods and raw materials.  
2. **Supplier management**: Store supplier lead times, automate reordering cycles, and track supplier performance.  
3. **Mobile app**: Launch a lightweight app for iOS and Android that provides restock alerts, quick inventory checks, and PO approvals on the go.  

**Expected Outcome:**  
By the end of Q3, Metric Mango should stand out from competitors by serving merchants with production needs and offering mobile-first convenience.

# Q4 – Monetization & Enterprise Readiness Upgrade Prompt

**Objective:**  
Monetize larger merchants and prepare Metric Mango for enterprise adoption.

**Detailed Prompt:**  
Launch the following upgrades:
1. **Tiered pricing model**:  
   - *Starter*: Current flat plan for small merchants.  
   - *Growth*: Includes multi-location support and PO automation.  
   - *Enterprise*: Includes advanced analytics, ERP integrations, and API access.  
2. **AI-driven forecasting**: Implement machine learning models to predict demand spikes based on seasonality, promotions, and historical sales patterns.  
3. **API access**: Provide enterprise merchants with API endpoints to integrate Metric Mango into custom workflows and systems.  

**Expected Outcome:**  
By the end of Q4, Metric Mango should be positioned as an enterprise-ready solution with scalable pricing, advanced forecasting, and extensibility through APIs.
